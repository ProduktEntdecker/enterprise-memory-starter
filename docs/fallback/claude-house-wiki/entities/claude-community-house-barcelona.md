# Claude Community House Barcelona

> Up: [Claude House wiki index](../index.md)

A four-day community programme run by sixteen community ambassadors at Carrer de Bilbao 128 in Poblenou, Barcelona.

## Facts

- Dates: 21 to 24 September 2026 (C01, 2026-09-13).
- Venue: Carrer de Bilbao 128, Poblenou, Barcelona. Lower two floors and the rooftop in use (C01, 2026-09-13).
- Sixteen community ambassadors from across Europe run the programme (C01, 2026-09-13).
- Independent community initiative, not an official Anthropic event (C01, 2026-09-13).
- Entry per session by QR code, through that session's own event page (C01, 2026-09-13).
- A session title naming a vendor programme describes the subject, not the organiser (C01, 2026-09-13).
- Hosts claim slots in the backoffice; an organiser releases the slot before the event page is requested (C03, 2026-09-13).
- Each ambassador runs at least one session of one to two hours, up to three slots (C03, 2026-09-13).
