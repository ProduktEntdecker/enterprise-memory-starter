# Rooftop

> Up: [Claude House wiki index](../index.md)

In use during the house days, but programmed by a separate organiser.

## Facts

- The rooftop programme is run by a separate organiser and is not part of the house programme (C01, 2026-09-13).
- No seat count in the backoffice, marked "special activations" (C02, 2026-09-19).
- Hours: the two sources disagree. C01 says from 18:00, C02 says only 16:00 to 18:00. See [FACTS.md](../FACTS.md) row 8, marked "needs decision".
