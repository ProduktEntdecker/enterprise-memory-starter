# Claude Community House Barcelona

> Up: [wiki index](../index.md)

A four-day community event, 21 to 24 September 2026, at Carrer de Bilbao 128 in
Poblenou, Barcelona.

## Facts

- **Dates: 21 to 24 September 2026** (C01, 2026-09-13)
- **Venue: Carrer de Bilbao 128**, Poblenou (C01)
- Lower two floors plus rooftop in use (C01)
- 16 ambassadors from across Europe run the programme (C01)
- **Not an official Anthropic event.** An independent community initiative (C01)

## How a session comes about

Claim a slot, wait for an organiser to release it, then request the event page and
send the link back (C03). Tickets are capped at room capacity plus 20 percent.

## Rooms

[Auditorium](auditorium.md), [Room 4](room-4.md),
[Workshops area](workshops-area.md), plus Rooms 1 to 3 and the rooftop.
