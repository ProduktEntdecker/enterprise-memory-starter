# C02 Rooms and capacity

> Up: [wiki index](../index.md)

## TL;DR

Seat counts for seven spaces, read from the planning backoffice on 19 September
2026. Two of them changed since the August list: Room 4 grew from 20 to 25, the
workshops area shrank from 120 to 96. The source states both changes explicitly,
so this is a supersession, not a contradiction.

## Key points

- Workshops area (cafeteria): 96 seats, ground floor, also used for drop-in sessions
- Room 1: 14 seats. Room 2: 16 seats. Room 3: 20 seats. Room 4: 25 seats
- Auditorium: 100 seats, level minus one, seated
- Rooftop: no seat count, only 16:00 to 18:00, special activations
- The August list is named as no longer valid
