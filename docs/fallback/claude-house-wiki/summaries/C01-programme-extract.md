# C01 Claude Community House Barcelona: public programme, extract

> Up: [Claude House wiki index](../index.md)

## TL;DR

Four days of community sessions, 21 to 24 September 2026, at Carrer de Bilbao 128 in Poblenou, Barcelona. Sixteen community ambassadors from across Europe run the programme on the lower two floors and the rooftop. The house is an independent community initiative, not an official Anthropic event, and entry to each session is by QR code from that session's own event page.

## Key points

- Dates: 21 to 24 September 2026. The extract lists sessions for Monday and Wednesday only.
- Venue: Carrer de Bilbao 128, Poblenou, Barcelona. Lower two floors and the rooftop in use.
- Sixteen community ambassadors from across Europe run the programme.
- Every session is registered through its own event page. Entry is by QR code.
- The house is an independent community initiative. It is not an official Anthropic event.
- A session title that names a vendor programme describes the subject, not the organiser.
- The rooftop programme from 18:00 is run by a separate organiser and is not part of the house programme.
