# C01 Public programme, extract

> Up: [wiki index](../index.md)

## TL;DR

Four days, 21 to 24 September 2026, at Carrer de Bilbao 128 in Poblenou. The lower
two floors and the rooftop are in use, and 16 ambassadors from across Europe run
the programme. The house states plainly that it is not an official Anthropic event.

## Key points

- Entry to every session is by QR code from that session's own event page
- Monday: a drop-in clinic 10:00 to 12:00, three sessions in the Auditorium and
  Room 4 between 11:00 and 16:00, a community session 16:00 to 18:00
- Wednesday: two sessions in Room 4, 13:00 to 15:00 and 15:00 to 16:00
- The rooftop programme from 18:00 is run by a separate organiser and does not
  belong to the house programme
