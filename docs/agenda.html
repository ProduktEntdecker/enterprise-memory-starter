Florian Steiner is a Claude Community Ambassador in Munich and helps agencies, consultancies and SMEs work productively with Claude.

Who this is for

Intermediate level. For people who already use Claude day to day and want a durable, reviewable knowledge base instead of one that resets every session: engineers, technical operators, and anyone maintaining docs, wikis or playbooks for an AI agent.

What you will leave with

  1. 1Understand why AI agents forget your business: it is a findability problem, not a memory problem.
  2. 2Compare the LLM wiki pattern against RAG, and see when each one fits, and why they complement each other.
  3. 3Watch the pattern work live: ingest a new source, then lint catches a contradiction, a duplicate and a stale fact.
