# Garbí

> Up: [Sotarena wiki index](../index.md)

Product collection of stackable aluminium bistro armchairs and tables for restaurant terraces.

## Facts

- Armchair frame: aluminium alloy 6063; weight 4.2 kg; stackable up to 10 high (S02, 2026-03).
- Available in 12 stock RAL colours (S02, 2026-03).
- Coating: polyester powder, salt-spray tested for 1,000 hours (ISO 9227) (S02, 2026-03).
- Warranty: 5 years on the frame, 2 years on the coating (S02, 2026-03).
- List prices 2026: armchair EUR 189; table 70 x 70 cm with HPL top EUR 310 (S03, 2026-01-12).
