# Garbí stackable armchair

**Product sheet** | Design and Product Development | Issue 03.2026 | Author: Oriol Pagès

Stackable aluminium bistro armchair for restaurant terraces.

## Technical data

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Frame | Aluminium alloy 6063 |
| Weight | 4.2 kg |
| Stacking | Up to 10 high |
| Colours | 12 stock RAL colours |
| Coating | Polyester powder, salt-spray tested for 1,000 hours (ISO 9227) |

## Warranty

- 5 years on the frame.
- 2 years on the coating.
