# S01 Board memo Q2 2026

> Up: [Sotarena wiki index](../index.md)

## TL;DR

The first half of 2026 closed below budget: revenue EUR 10.2 million against EUR 10.5 million, and gross margin 31.8% after 34.0% in 2025. Headcount was 86 on 2026-06-30, the EBITDA margin target of 11% by 2027 stays, and the board decides on powder coating capacity on 2026-10-15.

## Key points

- Revenue H1 2026: EUR 10.2 million; budget EUR 10.5 million.
- Gross margin H1 2026: 31.8%; full year 2025: 34.0%.
- Headcount on 2026-06-30: 86 employees.
- EBITDA margin target: 11% by 2027.
- The decision on powder coating capacity is scheduled for the board meeting on 2026-10-15.
