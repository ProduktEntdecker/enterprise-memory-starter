# S04 Capacity and lead times after summer

> Up: [Sotarena wiki index](../index.md)

## TL;DR

Operations sets the lead times after the summer: 6 weeks for stock colours and 8 weeks for custom RAL colours, and asks sales to use them in all quotes. The external powder coater is booked out until 2026-10-30, on-time delivery was 88% in July, and the Production Planner role is still vacant.

## Key points

- Standard lead time for stock colours: 6 weeks from order confirmation.
- Lead time for custom RAL colours: 8 weeks.
- Sales and customer service are asked to use these lead times in all quotes.
- The external powder coater, named "Color-Vall Coating" in the memo, is booked out until 2026-10-30.
- On-time delivery in July 2026: 88%.
- The Production Planner position has been vacant since 2026-06-15; the Head of Operations covers about 15 hours a week of planning.

## Notable quotes
