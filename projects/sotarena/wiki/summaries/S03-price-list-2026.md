# S03 Price list 2026

> Up: [Sotarena wiki index](../index.md)

## TL;DR

The price list 2026 is valid from 2026-01-12. It sets list prices for Garbí, Mistral and Llevant, a minimum order value of EUR 2,500, and a 12% surcharge for custom RAL colours from 50 pieces.

## Key points

- Valid from 2026-01-12.
- Garbí armchair EUR 189; Garbí table 70 x 70 cm with HPL top EUR 310.
- Mistral lounge chair EUR 520.
- Llevant sun lounger EUR 640.
- Minimum order value: EUR 2,500.
- Custom RAL colours: from 50 pieces, surcharge 12%.
