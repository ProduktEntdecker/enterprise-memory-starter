# S10 Approved supplier list

> Up: [Sotarena wiki index](../index.md)

## TL;DR

The approved supplier list, status 2026-09-01, names four key suppliers with register IDs. Alvenor supplies aluminium profiles (41% of purchasing spend), Colorvall coats all frames (18%), Nautifil supplies outdoor rope (7%) and Tabelux supplies HPL tabletops (6%), with a 7% price increase from 2026-10-01.

## Key points

- SUP-0102 Alvenor Extrusiones S.A.: aluminium profiles, 41% of purchasing spend.
- SUP-0417 Colorvall Coatings S.L.: powder coating of all frames, 18% of spend, located in Vallès Oriental.
- SUP-0533 Nautifil Cordas Lda.: outdoor rope, 7% of spend.
- SUP-0621 Tabelux Surfaces S.r.l.: HPL tabletops, 6% of spend, price +7% from 2026-10-01.
- The list is an extract of key suppliers, owned by Procurement.
