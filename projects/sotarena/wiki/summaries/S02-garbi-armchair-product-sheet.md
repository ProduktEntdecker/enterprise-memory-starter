# S02 Garbí stackable armchair: product sheet

> Up: [Sotarena wiki index](../index.md)

## TL;DR

The Garbí armchair is a stackable aluminium bistro armchair for restaurant terraces: alloy 6063 frame, 4.2 kg, stackable 10 high, 12 stock RAL colours. The polyester powder coating is salt-spray tested for 1,000 hours, and the warranty is 5 years on the frame and 2 years on the coating.

## Key points

- Frame: aluminium alloy 6063.
- Weight: 4.2 kg; stackable up to 10 high.
- Colours: 12 stock RAL colours.
- Coating: polyester powder, salt-spray tested for 1,000 hours (ISO 9227).
- Warranty: 5 years on the frame, 2 years on the coating.
