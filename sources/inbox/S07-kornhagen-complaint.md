# Complaint: Mistral lounge chairs, Baltic coast hotel

**From:** Dirk Albers, Head of Procurement, Kornhagen Hotels
**To:** Elena Vilaró, CEO, Sotarena S.L.
**Date:** 19.09.2026
**Subject:** Unacceptable: corrosion on 124 Mistral lounge chairs

Dear Ms Vilaró,

in April 2025 you delivered 124 Mistral lounge chairs to our hotel on the Baltic coast. The chairs now show blistering and white corrosion spots on the armrests and legs.

We expect:

1. a written action plan by 23.09.2026, and
2. full replacement of all 124 chairs by 01.03.2027.

The claim value at list price is EUR 64,480.

Until this is resolved, we put the 2027 framework agreement (EUR 1.1 million) on hold.

Regards,
Dirk Albers
Head of Procurement
Kornhagen Hotels
